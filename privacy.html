Privacy Policy

Hilum. Effective 27 September 2026.

Hilum collects nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third party code of any kind in the app, and the app contains no network code. What you log stays on your own devices.

What Hilum stores, and where

The foods you log, their portions, the limits you type and any note you add to one, your settings, and the products you keep from a shop shelf. All of it is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app, or Delete everything in Settings, deletes all of it from the phone.

iCloud

Your record is copied to your own private iCloud database so another device signed in to the same Apple Account can show it. That database belongs to your Apple Account and only your devices can read it. Hilum has no access to it and no server of its own.

The camera

When you scan a barcode or photograph an ingredient list or a Nutrition Facts panel, the picture is read on your phone to find the barcode or the printed words, and then discarded. No photograph is kept, and nothing is sent anywhere.

The food tables

Hilum ships the USDA FoodData Central tables inside the app. Searching them happens on your phone and asks nothing of any server.

Health information

What you log is information about your diet and is treated as sensitive. It is never sent, sold, shared or used for anything but showing it back to you.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Hilum never sees your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. It receives only whether a purchase is active.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

Two things, each started by you: the report for your dietitian as a PDF, and a spreadsheet of your record. Hilum makes the file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and Hilum is not involved.

Children

Hilum is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. Your record is on your devices and in your own iCloud, under your control: export it, delete it in Settings, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and any change that affects what Hilum collects will ship alongside an updated App Store privacy label.
