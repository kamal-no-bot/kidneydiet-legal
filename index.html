Hilum Support

Get in touch

Email support@madebykamal.app. A real person reads it, usually within two working days. Tell me your iOS version, the food you were looking at, and what you expected to see; a screenshot helps most.

Common questions

Why do the columns show foods instead of filling up?

A column fills against a limit, and Hilum never makes one up. Until you type the number your care team gave you, each column shows the foods that went into it. Tap + Limit at the top of a column, or open Settings, then Your limits. If nobody has given you a number yet, Settings has seven questions to take to your next appointment.

What does the plus after a number mean?

At least one food you logged carried no figure for that mineral, usually because a packaged label does not declare phosphorus. Hilum leaves the gap where it is rather than counting it as zero, so the total is a floor: the day held at least that much.

A number looks wrong

Every figure shows where it came from: the USDA reference tables, a product's own label, or a number you typed. Open the food and tap Where this comes from. If a USDA value looks wrong, send me the food's name and its USDA number and I will check it against the published record.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings, then Hilum Pro, and tap Restore at the foot of the screen. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, not to a device.

Moving to a new phone

Your record is kept on your phone and in your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone with iCloud on, and it follows you.
