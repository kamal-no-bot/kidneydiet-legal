What the numbers are

Figures come from the USDA FoodData Central tables, from what a product's label declares, or from what you type. Every figure in the app says which. Portions, recipes and products vary, and a label may not declare every mineral; where it does not, Hilum says so rather than guessing. Always follow the advice of your doctor, nurse or dietitian over anything the app shows.
